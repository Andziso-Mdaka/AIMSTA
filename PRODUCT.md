# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: South African companies buying professional training for their staff. The person on the site is typically an HR, L&D, or line manager (or a business owner in a smaller firm) evaluating a training provider for a team: what can be taught, how delivery works, and how to start a conversation. Individual professionals are a secondary audience.

## Product Purpose

AIMSTA is a professional training provider based in Pretoria, Gauteng. The site exists to make a company buyer confident that AIMSTA can upskill their people, and to get them to contact AIMSTA (enquiry form, email, or phone). Success is a qualified enquiry from an organisation.

## Positioning

Undecided in detail. Current site copy positions AIMSTA as practitioner-led, industry-aligned training ("trainers are practitioners, not academics"; curriculum refreshed with industry input). Treat these as the company's stated approach, not as verified proof.

## Operating Context

Buyers compare providers, often share links internally, and need something concrete to bring to a budget conversation: the training areas offered, delivery format, and a way to request a proposal or quote.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Training areas currently offered (from site copy): Technical Training (software, AI, cloud, data), Business Analytics, Digital Marketing, Soft Skills & Leadership, Project Management, Language Training.
- Example programmes on the site: Full-Stack Web Development, Data Science & Machine Learning, Digital Marketing Mastery.
- Stack: React 19 + Vite single-page app with in-app page switching (Home, About, Services, Courses, Contact). The contact form has no backend yet; it only shows a success state.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: AIMSTA. Tagline: "Efficacy for Excellence".
- The logo (`public/AIMSTA_LOGO_FULL.png`, `public/AIMSTA_LOGO_ONLY.png`) must be kept as-is. Every other visual choice (colour, type, layout) is open.
- Standing preference (chosen 2026-10-07): the site follows the category standard for professional / executive training, executed at full craft, not an unconventional visual concept. Quality bar: GIBS and UCT GSB Executive Education, and General Assembly Enterprise.

## Evidence on Hand

- Confirmed contact details: info@aimsta.com, +27 82 489 3294, Pretoria, Gauteng.
- All statistics (15K+ students, 98% success, 50+ trainers, 10+ years, 40+ countries), all testimonials, and all course prices and durations on the current site are **placeholders**. Do not present them as fact. Show them only as clearly marked placeholders, or leave space for real figures, until the owner supplies real ones.
- No client logos, case studies, or accreditations have been supplied. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. Speak to the organisation buying the training, not just to the individual learner.
2. Make the offer concrete: what is taught, how it is delivered, and how to start.
3. Never claim what has not been confirmed. Placeholders stay visibly placeholders.
4. One clear path to an enquiry from every page.
